# Glossary

Terms used across the Neural-Wave 2036 archive. *Diegetic* terms belong to the fictional world; *analytic* terms are the authors' framing.

## Diegetic terms

- **Neural-Wave** — the "empathic" AI+IoT care platform procured at scale. A camera-free home sensing system built on a 60 GHz millimeter-wave radar array, marketed as privacy-preserving.
- **Empathic AIoT** — AI-enabled Internet of Things that claims to infer well-being ("how you feel") from sensor data, rebranding inference as empathy.
- **mmWave / millimeter-wave radar** — radar in the 60 GHz band that captures involuntary micro-motions and vital micro-dynamics through walls and common household obstructions. The signature perceptual layer of Neural-Wave.
- **Micro-Doppler signature** — the spectral fingerprint produced by small periodic motions (heartbeat, respiration, tremor). The raw material from which Neural-Wave derives its inferences.
- **Spectrogram** — the time–frequency image Neural-Wave outputs instead of footage.
- **Risk score** — the abstract, hard-to-dispute output of Neural-Wave's inference layer.
- **Safety Lockdown Mode** — the system state triggered by a risk score breach: lights dim, the door locks, actuators act on the resident's behalf.
- **Stabilization Progress ring** — a wall-interface indicator that advances only when the body "agrees" with the model's prior. The UI of biometric compliance.
- **Pre-Stroke Agitation** — a diagnostic label whose signature (spiking Micro-Doppler + sharp HRV drop) Arthur's fandom happens to match.
- **"Hyper-Normal" sinus rhythm** — the idealized, perfectly regular signal a mimicry device outputs; more legible to the sensor than any real human heart.
- **Phantom** — the machine proxy that the sensing algorithm locks onto in the Degrade pathway.
- **NULL** — the data-void status of a body rendered spectrally nonexistent (e.g., inside a Faraday cage).
- **The Neural-Wave Quick Escape Manual** — the diegetic artifact: an "authorized" troubleshooting document that proceduralizes resistance through Comply / Degrade / Refuse.

## Analytic terms (authors' framing)

- **Spectral governance** — governance exercised through the frequency domain rather than through images; the shift from *being watched* to *being inferred*.
- **Infrastructural inference** — inference so embedded in domestic infrastructure that it becomes difficult to notice, interpret, or contest.
- **Accuracy Trap** — the assumption that improving inference fidelity resolves ethical tensions; the argument that deprivation of autonomy is often a *product* of accuracy, not a failure of it.
- **Performative Wellness** — coerced labor in which residents must continuously calibrate their bodies to satisfy the model's definition of "normality."
- **Coercive Legibility** — the feedback loop in which "passive" sensing actively disciplines bodies into standardized performance.
- **Adversarial Literacy** — the capacity to meaningfully obfuscate one's own data traces; a proposed digital right for the era of ubiquitous sensing.
- **Weapons of the weak** — everyday, low-visibility forms of resistance (after James C. Scott); the Manual as counterfeit paperwork.
- **Design fiction** — the method of creating discussable artifacts to surface tensions in plausible futures (see [design_rationale.md](design_rationale.md)).
- **Diegetic prototype** — an artifact embedded in a fictional world that makes a claim testable *inside* that world.

## The three pathways

- **Comply** — output acceptable calm on demand; earn permissions by demonstrating signal-level obedience.
- **Degrade** — crowd the system with a cleaner proxy than the self (data poisoning / mimicry device).
- **Refuse** — construct a spatial dead zone where inference cannot reach (Faraday-style shielding).
