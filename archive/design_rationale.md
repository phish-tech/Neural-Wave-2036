# Design Rationale

*Why this project is a "manual" and not an academic figure — and what that choice is for.*

## The problem the fiction addresses

HCI has extensively documented how care technologies *datafy* domestic life, and how camera visibility triggers resistance. The industry's answer — "camera-free" mmWave sensing — sidesteps the political problem by making the loss of privacy *invisible*. The shift from surveillance to **infrastructural inference** leaves residents with abstract risk scores they cannot interpret or dispute.

There is a gap: **no resident-facing tools exist to contest invisible inference.** Policy opt-outs assume a subject who knows what is happening and where to look. In the 2036 world, that subject no longer exists.

## Why design fiction

We follow the tradition of Blythe (research through design fiction) and Lindley & Coulton (fictional research papers): the artifact is the argument. Design fiction lets us make an invisible governance structure *visible and disputable* — not by describing it, but by building a world in which residents must live under it and survive it.

## Why a manual

The **Escape Manual** was chosen over a more conventional academic figure for three reasons:

1. **Genre does the work.** An "authorized" troubleshooting document is the resident-facing underside of algorithmic care. Its clinical voice — no outrage, no protest, only "standard procedure" — stages the satirical inversion at the heart of the argument: care has become infrastructural inference, and dignity is a maintenance task.
2. **Procedural, not pictorial.** A manual is read *to do something*. Comply / Degrade / Refuse are not illustrations of resistance; they are instructions for it. This makes the fiction's claim testable: the reader can ask, *would this work?* — and in asking, feel how little room the system leaves.
3. **A weapon of the weak disguised as paperwork.** Following Scott's "weapons of the weak," low-visibility, everyday forms of resistance are the realistic grammar of refusal for people with no leverage. The Manual makes refusal *actionable* rather than rhetorical.

## The three pathways as a design structure

The Manual compresses survival into three escalating moves, each answering a different question:

- **Comply** — *What if the body performs what the model demands?* Answers the Accuracy Trap by showing the cost of legibility-as-labor.
- **Degrade** — *What if a cleaner proxy crowds out the self?* Answers coercive legibility by attacking the sensor's "lock onto strongest signal" logic.
- **Refuse** — *What if the body stops producing data at all?* Answers the dignity deficit by engineering a data void.

Escalation matters: each pathway gives something up (dignity → privacy → participation), and the sequence is the argument.

## What the fiction is NOT saying

- It is **not** a technical guide. It deliberately omits frequencies, device models, and reproducible interference methods. (See the disclaimer in the README.)
- It is **not** an argument for an anti-technology future. It argues *for* friction, opacity, and the right to refuse — not against technology as such.
- It is **not** claiming mmWave is uniquely evil. It uses mmWave precisely because its "privacy-preserving" framing is so effective at erasing the politics of inference.

## Research-through-design notes (for reuse)

Methodological choices other researchers can borrow:

1. **Genre-as-argument**: choose a document genre whose conventions carry the critique (manual, SOP, service contract, EULA).
2. **Protagonist with dignity**: Arthur is not a victim to be rescued; his fandom is the plot engine. Design the scenario so the system is wrong *about something the reader values*.
3. **Escalating pathways**: structure refusal as a ladder (comply / degrade / refuse) so each artifact maps to a distinct conceptual claim.
4. **Diegetic UI**: the Stabilization Progress ring and "polite inevitability" chime show that governance also lives in the *interface*, not just the sensor.
5. **Ground it in real signal-processing logic**: Micro-Doppler, HRV, "lock onto strongest signal," and Faraday attenuation are all real phenomena — the fiction gains force by being technically plausible where it counts.

## What we hope the archive enables

That readers can walk into the 2036 world (timeline, glossary, setting), see the argument (manual, scenario cards), interrogate the threats (threat model), and reuse the method (this document) — while citing the work cleanly (CITATION.cff, citation.bib).
