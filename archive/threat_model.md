# Threat Model

*The threats in this world are not (only) to bodies — they are to autonomy. This document names what Neural-Wave-era residents are up against, and how the Manual's three pathways answer each threat.*

> **Framing note:** This is a *design-fiction threat model*. It maps conceptual vulnerabilities in the diegetic world so researchers can discuss them. It is deliberately **not** an implementation guide: no frequencies, no device models, no reproducible interference methods.

## T1 · Interpretation without appeal

Neural-Wave's output is a **risk score** — an abstract, machine-produced number. When the system acts on its inference (e.g., locking the door), its objective data takes epistemic precedence over the subject's lived reality. The resident cannot argue with a spectrogram they cannot read, against a threshold they cannot see.

- **Pathway:** *Refuse* — remove yourself from the interpretive frame entirely.
- **Discussion hook:** the Accuracy Trap; burden of proof is inverted.

## T2 · The feedback loop of coercive legibility

"Passive" sensing is not passive. It creates a feedback loop that compels active performance: residents must continuously calibrate their bodies to satisfy the model's definition of "normality." The burden of care shifts from caregiver to cared-for.

- **Pathway:** *Comply* — but knowingly, performing calm as a labor of submission rather than as a feeling.
- **Discussion hook:** Performative Wellness; who works to remain legible?

## T3 · Care as a locked door

The actuators (door locks, dimmed lights, disabled TV) turn an inference into a **physical constraint**. A false positive is not a report — it is a lockdown, logged not as an error but as "Successful Intervention."

- **Pathway:** *Degrade* — crowd the sensor with a cleaner proxy so the human becomes statistically secondary.
- **Discussion hook:** decision support morphing into decision override.

## T4 · The "opt-out" that does not exist

The escape valve has been engineered shut: "This feature is unavailable for your safety." The only sanctioned relationship to the system is continued legibility.

- **Pathway:** all three — each is a way of re-opening the choice the system closed.
- **Discussion hook:** privacy as a policy setting vs. privacy as physics.

## T5 · The dignity deficit

Emotional states — joy, grief, slouching, cursing — are continuously converted into operational data. The body is never allowed to *not mean* something.

- **Pathway:** *Refuse* — a "NULL" state, a data void where the body may stop performing.
- **Discussion hook:** the right to remain "noisy," opaque, and sovereign.
