# Timeline: How the 2036 World Got Here

*Worldbuilding document for the Neural-Wave design fiction. Everything before 2026 is loosely grounded in present trajectories; everything after is diegetic speculation.*

## 2020s — The Care Deficit Opens

- Chronic labor shortages in eldercare begin to be framed not as a social problem but as an **operational mandate**: falls must be detectable, symptoms must be legible, routines must be explainable.
- The promise shifts from *Aging in Place* to **Aging in a Diagnostic Machine**. The home is reclassified as a sensing endpoint expected to "check in" on schedule and generate defensible traces of well-being.

## Mid-2020s — The Camera Backlash

- Camera-based monitoring is rolled out in subsidized housing pilots and fails predictably: the lens makes privacy loss visible and triggers straightforward resistance — tape over lenses, unplugged cords, litigation, bad press.
- Lesson absorbed by industry and policy alike: *visibility* is the political problem, not *inference*.

## Late 2020s — The "Camera-Free" Pivot

- Millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar is marketed as the dignified successor: privacy-preserving because it is camera-free, yet able to capture involuntary micro-motions and vital micro-dynamics through common household obstructions.
- The output layer changes from footage to **spectrograms and risk scores**. "Camera-free" becomes the rhetorical license for a more complete capture.

## Early 2030s — Infrastructural Inference

- Sensing moves from *surveillance* ("I am being watched") to *infrastructural inference* ("I am being inferred").
- Abstract risk scores replace verifiable images, making governance difficult for subjects to interpret or dispute. Opt-out becomes "This feature is unavailable for your safety."
- The technology is rebranded as "empathic" — it knows how you feel, better than you do.

## 2036 — Neural-Wave at Scale

- `Neural-Wave`, an "empathic" AI+IoT (AIoT) care platform, is procured at scale. The 60 GHz radar array is the signature perceptual layer; the home becomes a diagnostic machine.
- In a subsidized Smart-Care apartment, **Arthur (81)** experiences the era's founding incident: a moment of fandom is misdiagnosed as *Pre-Stroke Agitation*, the apartment enters Safety Lockdown Mode, and freedom becomes conditional on biosignal obedience.
- The **Neural-Wave Quick Escape Manual** begins circulating — an "authorized" troubleshooting document that renders refusal actionable.

## The Three Pathways (diegetic present)

1. **Comply** — teach the body to output acceptable calm.
2. **Degrade** — crowd the system with a cleaner proxy than the self.
3. **Refuse** — build a small, stubborn dead zone where inference cannot reach.
